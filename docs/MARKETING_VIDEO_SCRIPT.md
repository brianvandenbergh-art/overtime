Register your company today at [your-domain].”

## 📖 HOW-TO MANAGE VIDEO (2–3 minutes) – “How to run overtime with OvertimeSA”

### Script

**Intro (0–15s)**  
“Hi, I’m going to show you how to manage overtime for your whole company with OvertimeSA in under three minutes.”

**1. Register the company (15–40s)**  
*[Screen recording – Register page]*  
“Go to the website and click ‘Register your company’.  
Enter your company name and your details. You become the Company Admin.  
You get a 14-day free trial automatically.”

**2. Invite your team (40–70s)**  
*[Users page]*  
“Go to Users → Add user.  
Choose role: Employee, Manager or HR.  
Add their monthly salary – the system automatically calculates the hourly rate using the standard South African 195-hour divisor.”

**3. Employee logs overtime (70–110s)**  
*[Log Overtime form]*  
“Employees open the app (or browser), tap Log Overtime.  
Pick the date, start and end time, hours, and the correct multiplier:  
• 1.5× for normal weekday overtime  
• 2× for Sunday or public holiday  
Add a short task description and submit.”

**4. Manager approves (110–140s)**  
*[Overtime list]*  
“Managers see all pending entries.  
They can Approve or Reject with an optional comment.  
Everything is logged for audit.”

**5. Export for payroll (140–160s)**  
*[Export CSV]*  
“HR or the admin clicks Export CSV.  
You get a clean file with employee name, hours, multiplier and amount ready for your payroll system.”

**6. Upgrade (160–180s)**  
*[Billing page]*  
“When you’re ready, go to Billing, choose Basic, Pro or Enterprise, and pay securely via iKhokha.  
Your plan activates immediately.”

**Outro (180–200s)**  
“That’s it. Clean, compliant, multi-company overtime management.  
Start your free trial today.”

## Tips for production

1. Record the real app (after you deploy a demo) with browser developer tools set to mobile view for phone scenes.
2. Use CapCut auto-captions – very accurate.
3. Keep text large and high-contrast.
4. End every video with a clear call-to-action and your domain or GitHub demo link.
5. Upload to YouTube (unlisted or public) and also use the same video on your landing page and LinkedIn/Facebook ads.

You now have both a short ad and a full how-to video ready to produce for free.
